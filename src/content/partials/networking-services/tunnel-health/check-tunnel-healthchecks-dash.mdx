Your traffic may only be routed through a subset of data centers. Refer to the **Traffic volume (1 hour)** column for aggregate traffic to your connector and click on the connector name for data center health and traffic data.

## Check connector health:

1. Go to the [Cloudflare dashboard](https://dash.cloudflare.com/login) and select your account.
2. Go to Networking > Insights > Network Health.
3. The page shows the top 3 connectors based on traffic and a table with all the connectors in your account. Click on a connector to view health checks run from Cloudflare data centers and traffic volume by direction. By default, only data for data centers with traffic are shown but you can change the filter to "All data centers" to access diagnostics for data centers without traffic.
5. To troubleshoot issues between Cloudflare and your origin network, select **Run traceroute**. If the option is disabled, confirm with your administrator that you have the necessary permissions and that you are targeting a public IP.
